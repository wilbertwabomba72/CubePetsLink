Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

Cube Pets Link is an ad-supported puzzle game. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

The game does not require an account, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or camera access. Advertising partners may use device identifiers, including the advertising identifier when permitted, to deliver and measure ads.

Advertising and Tracking Permission

On iOS 14 and later, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking-related advertising initialization should occur. If you deny permission, the game remains playable and ads should be served without using the IDFA for tracking.

Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress, high scores, star ratings, and audio settings locally on your device. This data is used only to keep your game progress and preferences.

Third Parties

Ads may be provided by third-party advertising partners. These partners may process device identifiers and ad performance information for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement, subject to your ATT choice and applicable law.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly request personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be made available with a new effective date.
